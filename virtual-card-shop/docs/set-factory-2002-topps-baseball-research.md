# 2002 Topps Baseball — unreleased Set Factory draft

Completed catalogue inventory: **3,761 card entries in 49 families**, including the ordinary **718 base positions** and **39 true base RCs**. This covers all 46 related-set namespaces (base, 41 inserts, three parallels, one promo) and three additional optional variation pools. The product is **unreleased**; no production database was accessed and nothing was deployed.

## Inventory

Optional variations are separate so ordinary base completion does not demand all 73 Barry Bonds versions. They are genuine catalogued variants, not invented extra numbered positions. The main Base and Limited families each use HR73 as the representative #365. Base #446 represents the pack error; its factory-set correction remains in Base Variations. HTA represents the corrected Pujols #160, with the alternative error in its variation pool. This is a reversible Admin draft choice, documented before release.

## Sources and complete-row audit

The committed source snapshot contains **86 parsed source entries**, original URLs, captured HTML SHA-256 hashes and reviewed parsed-record digests, plus **17 original image hashes**. The generator fetches all primary pages, follows only the explicitly reviewed shared-program redirect, checks namespaces and card IDs, and requires the complete actual row counts. It compares every one of the **1,421 independent MyCardBatch number/name/team tuples**, all 791 detailed BCP base entries including variants, complete separate RC inventory, all parallel identities/teams, and all 330 buyback signer/year identities. Reviewed data changes fail closed and require renewed research; no silent source fallback or guessed rows.

Primary evidence:

- [TCDB base](https://www.tcdb.com/Checklist.cfm/sid/1562/2002-Topps?PageIndex=1), all eight pages: 791 detailed rows covering 718 slots, 72 additional HR variants and one Loretta correction. [Rookie index](https://www.tcdb.com/Rookies.cfm/sid/1562/2002-Topps?PageIndex=1): all 39 true RCs, also corroborated by BCP detailed RC flags.
- [Complete insert/parallel/promo index](https://www.tcdb.com/Inserts.cfm/sid/1562/2002-Topps): all 45 related namespaces plus base. Full Gold seven pages, HTA seven pages, Limited eight pages, buybacks four pages, and all remaining insert pages were actually retrieved.
- [BaseballCardPedia](https://baseballcardpedia.com/index.php/2002_Topps): detailed base/insert rosters, physical odds by format/series, errors, materials, factory-set scope and historical scarcity.
- [MyCardBatch](https://mycardbatch.com/checklists/topps-2002-baseball): 1,421 tuples in 41 tables. It does not provide full parallel or variation masters and its vintage buyback table contains only 233 entries. It is supplementary evidence, not an assumed complete master.
- [Cardboard Connection](https://www.cardboardconnection.com/2002-topps-baseball-cards): full 330-item vintage buyback checklist and additional complete insert rosters. Cataloguing abbreviations and card prefixes are explicitly reconciled, not matched fuzzily.

### Numbering and roster resolutions

- **#7 does not exist**. Base #1–719 excluding #7 gives 718 positions. Series One is 364 positions; Series Two is 354. The foil subsets are actual base cards, not invented short-print families.
- **Bonds #365 has 73 actual HR versions**. Every version was retrieved, independently matched to its HR number, and retained across Base/Limited. Catalogue A…BU suffixes are variation identifiers, not additional physical printed numbers. One HR73 exists in HTA.
- **Gold is 660 cards**, not 718: #1–331 excluding #7 and #366–695 only, each serial /2002. No Gold #365 or foil-only ranges are fabricated.
- **HTA has 685 distinct stamped positions**, plus the optional Pujols error. The 33 unstamped Series One foil cards (#332–364) are identical to base and are not duplicated as purported stamped HTA cards.
- **Limited is a 1,950-set production run**, not serial /1950. No fictional /27 serial numbers are assigned to the 73 scarce Bonds versions.
- **330 vintage buyback identities are completed**. TCDB's header says 330, but its actual pages contain 329. The omitted card is Whitey Ford's 1962 In Action card: CBC `WF10 Whitey Ford 62 IA` and BCP `Whitey Ford 62 IA 100` independently establish its buyback inclusion; [Dean's original-card catalogue](https://www.deanscards.com/p/40148/1962-Topps-315-Ford-Tosses-a-Curve-Whitey-Ford) confirms printed #315. This is not an invented row to satisfy a count. BCP's 329-line list instead omits Bill Mazeroski 1971, which TCDB and CBC supply; its duplicate Perry 1966 year also differs from the 1979 entry in the other two catalogues.
- Vintage printed numbers repeat across years and sometimes the same multi-player original appears with different signers. Local IDs use year/brand/printed-number/CID for 329 TCDB entries, with every original number/year preserved in metadata. Ford's independent addition uses `1962-TOPPS-315`. No catalogue-generated IDs are falsely described as physical printed codes.
- The plain autograph family is **40**, not an invented 41st Alex Rodriguez card. BCP's separate TA-AR series-two listing duplicates the TA19 subject/issue represented in the complete TCDB/MyCardBatch roster. Original fulfilled cards remain; generic expired redemption tickets are not additional signed cards.
- Blue, Gold and Red checklist families each have eight cards. Repeated printed 1–4 numbers use explicit local S1/S2 prefixes; their checklist coverage stays in variant. Their 24 blank teams are intentional checklist exceptions.
- Team stickers, Logo Race and Ticket to History cards are actual catalogued unnumbered collector cards. Local NNO-CID keys prevent collisions. Historic contest/fulfillment rights are not implied.
- The shared 2001-copyright Legends link SID539727 redirects to canonical SID13056; this exact mapping is validated. Keep its 69-entry master distinct from the 24-entry 2002-copyright master. Neither family implies that every subject was eligible in every 2002 flagship wrapper. Do not import all later 113 advertised cross-year subjects simply to inflate a 2002 pool.

### Names, teams, RCs and relic provenance

- Exactly **39 base RC suffixes** are applied. Mauer #622, Bay #326, Freddy Sanchez #313 and Rich Harden #687 are included. Harden prints James; display uses Rich Harden with the printed-name note. ASR cup, Draft Picks marketing, rookie reprint and ROY award tags never create a true RC.
- Every output name is stripped of separate source tokens, with descriptors in subset/variant. Tests preserve suffix-looking surnames Boswell, Hall and Marshall. Historical clubs are retained, including Brooklyn, New York Giants, Cincinnati Redlegs, Kansas City Athletics and Houston Colt .45s.
- Original Like Father Like Son scans correct **Alomar** to Sandy Sr. / Sandy Jr. / Roberto and **Braves / White Sox / Indians**. Source catalogues incorrectly list only Atlanta. Original **Boone** scans show Bob / Aaron / Bret and **Phillies / Reds / Mariners**, correcting the source's duplicated Reds affiliation. Material order is aligned to pictured subjects; Cruz Sr. is bat and Cruz Jr. jersey. Berra/Bonds are described as used uniforms without inventing a more specific provenance than the original back establishes.
- Limited #340 incorrectly names Aaron Boone; independent base/Gold/HTA and the league-leader roster establish **Bret Boone**. Limited #345 team ordering is reconciled to the printed subjects. The Mets/Pirates tribute #358 retains both clubs rather than the incomplete base-source Mets-only team.
- BCP's United We Stand entries name pictured players while TCDB describes the games. Both are retained as researched representations; BCP #357's Astros claim conflicts with the actual Diamondbacks/Rockies event and is not copied as a team.
- Stadium-seat relics are **stadium seats**, not player-used equipment. The unsigned Rizzuto/Pafko dual has a reported production of 52 but is not serial numbered, despite TCDB's SN52 tag. Snider/Berra is hand /52. Signed duals are hand /25.
- Pine Tar /200 and Trademark /100 are hand-numbered; Barrel /50 is serial numbered. `TH2` and 1952 signed-reprint `PRA2` are retained as catalogue disambiguators; ambiguous exact physical suffixes are not invented.
- Signed vintage buybacks use hand /100, /200 or /300 and Topps authentication holograms. Multi-player cards preserve which player actually signed; they are not represented as all-subject dual/triple autographs.

## Remaining verification blockers — keep unreleased

These are specifically identified uncertainties, not claimed completed scan verification:

1. **Prime Cuts signed bat serial numbering.** [Beckett's programme description](https://www.beckett.com/baseball/2002/topps-prime-cuts-autograph-relics) corroborates eight signed relic subjects with published /60 Series One and /50 Series Two limits. TCDB omits AU/SN tags and calls them jumbo bat swatches. The named catalogue family and independent descriptions establish signatures, but [a reported Bonds original example is /47](https://www.ebay.com/itm/186244391284), conflicting with /60. The seller page's original images could not be retrieved (access challenge), so the reported example is not claimed visually verified. Variants explicitly label **published limits** and unresolved actual denominators. No fabricated final SN60/SN50 flags.
2. **Paul O'Neill 5K-PO exact material.** Full checklist identity/number/team are corroborated, but no original scan or reliable material description was retrieved. The CSV states memorabilia with exact material unverified. It does not guess bat versus jersey.
3. **Exact printed suffixes and corrected-error imagery.** Catalogue variant identifiers are distinguished from confirmed printed numbers. The retrieved HTA Pujols source image assignment does not independently establish the corrected-back identity; retain catalogue COR metadata and review original images before harvesting that variant. No existing card images are overwritten.
4. **Mixed series/format odds and cross-product availability.** Gold's S1 1:19 versus S2 1:12 cannot be one verified integer denominator. Mixed-series signed reprints/Prime Cuts, factory-only/retail-only families, optional variants and shared Legends pools stay null pending eligibility QA. Yankee seat Hobby 1:579 versus the published Retail 1:4313/HTA 1:1472 needs wrapper corroboration and stays null. Summer School Two Bagger uses 1:3733 from TCDB and BCP's detailed text; overview/CBC 1:3373 discrepancy remains documented for wrapper QA.

All numbered subjects and collector inventories are present, but these material/serial/physical-eligibility uncertainties mean the product is **not ready for release**. It is an Admin draft for pricing and image harvesting.

## VCS pack conventions

Hobby draft: **36 packs × 10 nominal cards**. Physical HTA was 12 × 37; factory sets and retail-exclusive relics are not treated as Hobby pack inserts. The two physical series remain one base collector checklist. Known series-native insert rates are explicitly flattened over the combined draft rather than falsely described as exact original box collation. Set-wide odds live on ProductSets only.

Null non-base odds are excluded by `src/app/api/rip/open/route.ts` and `src/lib/pack-pricing.ts`. The engine does not reproduce all original box/set assortments or guarantee series-specific variant availability. Price, pack art, card images and release QA remain pending. No pack engine change is included in this data-only draft.
