## Historical teams, names and physical checklist cards

All 1,270 player-card rows have actual historical franchise values. This is not an update to later 1999 rosters: Roger Clemens remains Toronto, David Wells is a Yankee and Randy Johnson is Arizona in this issue. These affiliations agree across all four numbered source lists and the supplementary team listing. Every insert's team also agrees with its corresponding base subject.

Base #30 Will Clark is an important pictured-uniform distinction. The original back shows a Texas Rangers uniform and a **Baltimore Orioles** logo, matching the source team. Preserve Baltimore as team and put the Rangers-uniform note in variant, never in the player name. Do not replace the franchise from the pictured jersey. That detail is added only to the inspected base card, rather than inventing notes for uninspected parallel backs.

[TCDB Checklists](https://www.tcdb.com/Checklist.cfm/sid/125536/1999-SkyBox-Thunder-Checklists) contains two distinct two-sided unnumbered cards: #1–230 and #231–300 plus inserts. The second card's back lists all six named insert families, including Hip-No-Tized. Stable local identifiers NNO-A and NNO-B distinguish the two real cards without pretending those IDs were printed. Player is `Checklist`, subset is `Checklist`, and printed coverage belongs in variant. The two blank teams are intentional and allowed by the checklist exception. `review.teamData=COMPLETE` therefore requires no pending-team override.

Clean player fields exclude RC-unrelated source tags, serial runs, sample labels, checklist ranges, descriptive captions and uniform notes. Collector numbers retain the parenthesized suffix convention such as `1 (ID)` or `1 (D)`. No insertion odds appear in per-card variants. No unlisted corrected-error variations are invented; TCDB's Errors page currently lists none.

The Ben Grieve #247 dealer/media **Promotional Sample** is excluded. Canonical regular #247 remains in base and all three parallels. No pack autograph, memorabilia or separate hologram checklist is verified; foil finishes alone do not establish an additional issue. ## Hobby configuration and odds limitations

Hobby default: **eight cards per pack / 36 packs per box**, single series. Original Hobby wrapper and box were reviewed; the wrapper identifies eight cards, Hobby packaging and both rare foil parallels. Catalog descriptions, TCDB trivia and indexed retailer Hobby details agree on 36 packs. The inspected retail side panel directly confirms Rant 1:2 and the printed insert rates it lists.

| Family | Physical evidence | Default Hobby oddsPerPack |
|---|---|---:|
| Unleashed | 1:6 | 6 |
| www.batterz.com | 1:18 | 18 |
| In Depth | 1:24 | 24 |
| Hip-No-Tized | Hobby 1:36 | 36 |
| Turbo Charged | 1:72 | 72 |
| Dial 1 | 1:300 | 300 |
| Rant | Retail-only 1:2 | null (not a Hobby pull) |
| Rave | Hobby /150; exact denominator unknown | null |
| Super Rave | Hobby /25; exact denominator unknown | null |
| Checklists | Randomly packed out; exact denominator unknown | null |

The six Hobby rates agree across the canonical related-set notes, BCP and indexed Hobby descriptions. Rave/Super Rave print runs do not justify invented pack odds. Checklists are real pack-issued cards, but “randomly issued” does not provide an exact denominator. Those three families remain inactive in pack pulls until verified odds are configured. Rant is also staged for pricing and image work, with null odds specifically because the product's default configuration is Hobby. Do not silently inject a retail-only parallel into Hobby packs.

Two retail discrepancies were identified and are not treated as settled facts: BCP says 36 packs for both channels, while current TCDB trivia says retail 20; and inspected retail packaging advertises **five** insert families, omitting Hip-No-Tized, although BCP assigns that insert a retail rate. The actual checklist card lists all six insert families across the product, but does not prove retail channel availability. This bundle models Hobby, where the six-family scope and rates agree. It makes no definitive retail Hip-No-Tized or exact retail-box simulation claim.

VCS's independent ProductSet rolls replace base slots; card positions, precise within-pack tier collation and hit correlations are not reproduced. Product remains `released:false`, with unknown odds, pack QA, images and pricing pending. The initial zero pack price is an unpriced draft value, not an asserted retail price. No production database was opened or modified.

## Validation and reproducibility

The delivered artifacts are the actual 1,272-row cards CSV, DRAFT bundle, full parsed source snapshot, this audit, standalone Python generator and actual-importer integration test. Generator checks exact complete number sets, unique identities, every subject/team, source namespaces, tier ranges, true RC inventory, /150 and /25 runs, parallel correspondence, two allowed generic team blanks and ProductSet-only odds. Every linked source card row must parse; unexpected numbering or missing source data fails before atomic output replacement.

Live refresh retrieves every required source, reparses the independent lists and supplementary team listing, and checks the hashes of seventeen reviewed original scans. An observed temporary checklist HTTP 503 prompted bounded retries for transient 429/502/503/504 or connection failures. Three attempts maximum; permission errors such as 403 fail immediately. Retry tests simulate recovery after a 503, immediate 403 failure and exhaustion after three 503s. Validation is never bypassed or replaced with invented source data.

Parser tests cover caption separation, whole trailing-token boundaries, suffix-looking surnames, parenthesized IDs, unnumbered cards, wrong namespaces and unknown linked card rows. Fifteen corrupted source cases must reject: missing/duplicate base, wrong player/team, blank player team, false RC/index, wrong serial runs, invented Rant serial attribute, truncated Rant, incorrect insert numbering, missing checklist, wrong namespace and odds leaked into variants.

Rerun creates no duplicates, updates all 1,272 cards and preserves sentinel prices/book values and front/back/pack images. Released remains false and a released manifest rejects. All 30 existing unit tests, pack metadata tests, checklist grade tests and CSV import smoke test passed; integration-test ESLint passed. These checks used no production database or deployment.
